About

Design is a business decision. I treat it like one.

Most designers start with how things should look. I start with what the business needs to happen — a booking, a purchase, a signup — and work backwards through research, strategy, and interface design until that outcome becomes the path of least resistance.

I've designed ecommerce stores, travel platforms, real-estate portals, SaaS products, and complete brand systems for clients in the United Kingdom, Australia, Qatar, and Nepal. As an entrepreneur myself, I understand what's actually at stake behind every screen: revenue, trust, and time.

The process

Seven steps.
Zero guesswork.

A repeatable system refined across ecommerce, SaaS, and service businesses. You always know what's happening, why, and what comes next.

Start with a call
01

Discover

Your business model, goals, and what a "win" looks like in numbers — before a single screen exists.

02

Research

Users, competitors, and the moments in your current funnel where trust breaks and visitors leave.

03

Strategy

Information architecture, conversion paths, and messaging hierarchy — the blueprint every design decision answers to.

04

Wireframes

Low-fidelity structure to validate flows fast and cheaply, so we change ideas — not finished screens.

05

Prototype

High-fidelity, interactive design — real content, real motion, real feel. What you approve is what ships.

06

Testing

Usability checks and refinement against the goals we set in step one. Opinions lose; evidence wins.

07

Launch

Developer-ready handoff or full delivery — then measurement, iteration, and support as the numbers come in.

Questions

Answers, upfront.

Who is Shreejesh Raj Joshi?

A business-focused product and UI/UX designer partnering with founders and teams in the UK, Australia, Europe, and North America. Also an entrepreneur — which is why every design decision is made with revenue, trust, and growth in mind.

What industries do you design for?

SaaS, ecommerce, travel and tourism, real estate, healthcare and clinics, NDIS providers, and service businesses. If your business depends on people trusting a screen enough to act, that's my territory.

Why does your design increase conversions?

Because it starts with your funnel, not with aesthetics. Layout, hierarchy, copy, and interaction are engineered around proven UX principles — Hick's Law, Fitts's Law, the Peak–End Rule — so the next step is always obvious, and always easy.

What does a typical engagement look like?

A seven-step process from discovery to launch: strategy first, wireframes to validate cheaply, high-fidelity prototypes, testing, and a developer-ready handoff or full delivery. You see progress at every stage.

How do we start?

Book a strategy call. We'll review your goals and your current experience, and you'll leave with a concrete recommendation — no obligation.
