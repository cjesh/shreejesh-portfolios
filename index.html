About

A designer who codes.
A founder's mindset.

Shreejesh Raj Joshi is a multidisciplinary designer and entrepreneur from Kathmandu, Nepal, working at the intersection of design, technology, and business.

Trained as a graphic designer and self-taught as a frontend developer, Shreejesh has spent the last several years designing and shipping digital products for startups and established businesses across Nepal, the United Kingdom, and Australia. His expertise spans UI/UX design, product design, frontend engineering, brand identity, and design systems.

His design approach is research-driven and outcome-focused. He treats every project as a product problem first and a visual problem second — beginning with the user, defining the real question, and shaping interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and built to scale. He believes great products aren't only beautiful; they're useful, scalable, and built with intent.

As an entrepreneur, Shreejesh brings business thinking to the design table — making decisions that serve users, founders, and growth simultaneously. He works with founders to validate ideas, design MVPs, and ship production-ready experiences.
